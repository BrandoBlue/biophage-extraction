# BIOPHAGE: EXTRACTION v0.2.0 "Larva"

**Play:** https://claude.ai/artifact/36F22AJow4EAKNTJyrgUhE · or download `biophage-0.2.0.html` below and double-click it (Chrome or Edge).

Larva gives you a reason to extract. What you bring home stays in your stash, you can sell it or spend it in the Hive, and dying costs you what you carried.

## New this build
- **The Hive.** A main menu with your host turning on its pedestal. From there: host select, stash and loadout, vendors, raid select.
- **Persistent stash.** Loot you extract is saved, and stays after you close the page. Load it into your internal cavities for the next raid. The gut pouch is a secure slot that survives death.
- **Death has teeth.** Die and you lose everything in your cavities. The gut pouch and the Synapse Burster come home. A Heart Husk in your cavities revives you once.
- **Vendors.** Mother Clot and the Archivist buy, sell and offer contracts. Your reputation with each one unlocks better stock. There is also the Flesh Market, where prices shift with the market's mood.
- **Contracts.** The first two chains. Progress counts when you extract, and rewards and unlocks are applied on the spot.
- **A second zone.** The Wreck of the Long Seed: a crashed seed-ship with interior rooms. The Archivist's *Signal in the Wreck* contract unlocks it. Raid select lists every zone and how to get into it.
- **Five Abominations** hunt the island. Each one has its own body, behaviour and weak point: Needlemaw, Glasswalker (nearly invisible until it moves), Oculimoth swarms (they screech for friends), Mournback (armoured front, flank its spore vents) and the Bile Cantor. Limbs can be severed.
- **The Brood Heart.** A car-sized heart nest in a fungal crater. It wakes when you come close and sends out six waves. Shoot its four ventricles. Killing it opens the Brood Cache.
- **The Reliquary.** A 10 to 12 m colony creature in the sunken Reliquary Bowl. It stalks you, blooms and collapses.
- **Corpse harvesting.** Hold E on a dead Abomination to cut out its organ. Organs are your main source of loot.
- **Viruses.** Inject Plague-Stalker, Adrenaline or Spliced Parasites mid-raid. Your arms visibly mutate into the new strain.
- **Combat additions.** Melee (V), Neuro-Spike grenade (G), Maw-Seed mine (X), and a Psych-Wave Pulse that reveals hidden things.
- **Structures.** Enterable buildings on the island: the Hollow Spire, the Long Seed debris field, grown walls and interiors. Seed Spire extracts shift between raids.
- **Look.** Ethereal, not neon: restrained glow, a hazier sky, softer colour, gore on hits, spore beam tracers, and the mutation dissolve.

## Changed
- The island has more sites: the Brood Heart crater, Gene Bloom harvest nodes and a Gravity Bloom anomaly. A second Seed Spire opens 4:00 into the raid.
- TAB opens your cavities in the raid. The game keeps running while it is open.

## Fixed
- The camera no longer jumps to the sky when you click to take control.
- The click-to-take-control box no longer covers the Biomass bar.

## Known issues
- The Hive Body (Gestation Vat, Spore Garden, Memory Coral), the fungal caves and the Seed-Key lockers in the Wreck come in the next build.
- Only the Neuro-Infiltrator is playable. Wraith and Behemoth arrive in Host.
- Single player only. Multiplayer comes later.

## Controls
Click to take control. WASD move, Shift sprint, C slide or crouch, Space jump, Esc release the mouse.
Left mouse fire, right mouse aim, R regrow, F Ego Charge, V melee, 1 and Q Psych-Wave Pulse, 2 Clot Gel, G grenade, X mine, hold E harvest, TAB cavities.
