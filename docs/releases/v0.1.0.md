# BIOPHAGE: EXTRACTION v0.1.0 "Spore"

The first playable raid. Drop onto a Zone 7 island as the Neuro-Infiltrator, fight through the Abominations, tear open the Bio-Pod and get out through the Seed Spire before the zone collapses. Die and everything you carried is gone.

**Play:** open the play link, or download `biophage-0.1.0.html` below and double-click it (Chrome or Edge, any recent GPU).

## New this build
- **Zone 7 island:** a bioluminescent fungal island under a void-indigo sky, ringed planet, nebula, liquid-light falls and drifting spores.
- **Neuro-Infiltrator host:** walk, sprint, jump. Biomass (health) with hit zones, Neural Overload heat that locks you out of sprint and abilities when it maxes.
- **Synapse Burster:** living spore gun firing real projectiles with spread and falloff, regrowing magazine, Ego Charge that fills on hits and launches six seeking spores.
- **Abilities:** Psych-Wave Pulse (1 or Q) and Clot Gel healing (2).
- **Two Abominations:** the Needlemaw, a fast pack crawler that lunges, and the Bile Cantor, a hovering spitter with three glowing song pustules to pop. Both hear your gunfire and footsteps, and bleed glowing fluid when hit.
- **Loot:** one Bio-Pod guarded by a Cantor; hold E to tear it open.
- **Extraction:** hold the Seed Spire zone for 8 seconds. The 10-minute raid clock warns at 2:00, then the zone collapses and eats your Biomass.
- **Results:** EXTRACTED shows your haul value in DNA; HOST LOST shows what you lost. Deploy again from either.
- **HUD:** Biomass and Overload bars, eyeball portrait, Psych-Wave Scanner pointing to the extract, ability slots, ammo and Ego Charge, raid timer, carried loot.

## Known issues
- Drifting spores can draw as small squares on some graphics drivers (fix in progress).
- The "click to enter" prompt shows faintly behind the result screen.
- The zone-collapse warning is hard to read against bright fungus.
- No audio yet, and no menus beyond the result screen (both come in later builds).

## Controls
Click to take control. WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · Left mouse fire · Right mouse aim · F or middle mouse Ego Charge · R reload · 1 or Q Psych-Wave Pulse · 2 Clot Gel · hold E open Bio-Pod · Esc release the mouse.
