# 01 Core loop

## Raid loop (minutes)
**Drop → Scout → Fight / Loot → Decide → Extract or die.**

1. **Drop.** The host is fired into Zone 7 inside a seed capsule and lands at a spawn point with 8 s of safety (no enemy within 35 m).
2. **Scout.** The Psych-Wave Scanner shows the extract direction and, after a Psych-Wave Pulse, nearby life. Bioluminescent landmarks (falls, giant fungi, crashed bio-tech) are the navigation language.
3. **Fight and loot.** Abominations guard the good stuff. Kills bank DNA; containers give items. Every shot and every opened container makes noise that pulls more creatures in.
4. **Decide.** Every extra minute adds loot and adds reinforcements. The core tension is "one more pod".
5. **Extract.** Reach the Seed Spire and hold its zone for the channel time while the Spire's signal draws everything nearby. Leave the zone and the channel resets.
6. **Death.** Everything carried is lost, including kill DNA.

## Meta loop (days)
**Extract → Bank → Evolve → Outfit → Deploy.** Extracted DNA buys mutations on the Genetic Synapse tree and trades at the Flesh Market; items go to the Stash; viruses get integrated in the Viral Lab. Deploying risks whatever you bring. (Larva and later, see [06](06-roadmap-systems.md).)

## What Spore ships
Only the raid loop, single-player, Neuro-Infiltrator only, no persistence. The end screen shows **EXTRACTED** with haul value in DNA, kills and time, or **HOST LOST** with what was lost. A "Deploy again" button restarts the raid. That is enough to feel the bet: you watch your haul number die with you.

## Pacing targets (Spore)
| Play style | Target length | How it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rush | about 60 to 75 s | Sprint-manage across the island, dodge one Needlemaw pack, extract with kill DNA only |
| Full raid | about 2 to 4 min (the playtest bot, which beelines with good aim, does it in 75 s) | Clear the Cantor guarding the Bio-Pod, loot it, fight through reinforcements, extract |
| Greedy | 8 to 10 min | Farm reinforcement waves for DNA until the zone collapses at 10:00 |
