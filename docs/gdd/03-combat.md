# 03 Combat

## Biomass (health)
Biomass is your body mass. Damage tears it away; there is **no natural regeneration** in a raid. Heal with Clot Gel (70 over 3 s, slows you to 60% while applying). Hit zones on hosts: head ×1.8, torso ×1.0, limbs ×0.75. Limb damage states (crippled leg slows, crippled arm adds sway) arrive in Larva.

## Neural Overload (the energy meter)
A **heat meter, 0 to 100, that fills** and decays. It is the price of doing anything fast.
- Fills: sprint 9/s, jump 4, Psych-Wave Pulse 25, Ego Charge 30, Cantor death cloud 10/s.
- Decays 16/s after 1 s without filling (half speed in toxic gas for the Infiltrator).
- **At 100 the host Overloads** for 2.5 s: cannot sprint or use abilities, heavy chromatic aberration and scanner static, a ringing tone. Then the meter resets to 60.
- From cold you can sprint about 11 s before Overloading. Good play is pulsing sprint, not holding it.

HUD: Overload bar glows violet, pulses above 75, and the eyeball portrait dilates and twitches as it rises.

## Synapse Burster
A living spore gun, the host's first weapon. Fleshy body, three tracking eyes that look where you aim, tentacle feed tubes into the host's forearm, violet crystal implant that glows with Ego Charge.
- **Primary fire:** fast projectile spores (90 m/s, slight drop) at 8 per second, 14 damage each plus a 4-damage burst within 0.8 m. Not hitscan: at 40 m you lead slightly.
- **Magazine:** 42 spores. Reserve is infinite in Spore: reload is "regrow" (2.2 s), the gun visibly swells. Ammo as a lootable resource arrives in Larva.
- **Falloff:** full damage to 35 m, down to 60% at 70 m.
- **Weak points** (glowing nodes on creatures) take ×2.0.
- **Aim down sights:** the gun's eyes converge; spread drops from 1.6° to 0.35°. Cannot fire while sprinting.
- **Ego Charge (alt fire):** the crystal fills 1 per hit, 2 per weak-point hit. At 30 it is ready (charge ring on the weapon panel). Release: 6 seeking spores, 30 damage each, 2.5 m bursts, fills Overload by 30. It is the answer to a Needlemaw pack or the finisher on a Cantor.

Time to kill, close range body shots: Needlemaw about 0.75 s, Bile Cantor about 2 s. A magazine kills a 6-pack with no misses.

## Abilities
Five HUD slots. Spore uses two.
1. **Psych-Wave Pulse** (Infiltrator): a ring of psychic light expands to 40 m; every living thing in range is outlined and pinned on the scanner for 4 s. Within 6 m it deals 10 and staggers. Costs 25 Overload, 12 s cooldown.
2. **Clot Gel** (consumable): heal 70 over 3 s.
Slots 3 to 5 show locked mutation sockets (they fill with viruses and mutations from Larva).

## Feedback rules
- **No floating damage numbers.** Hit-markers only: a small glowing cross; brighter and with a wet crack sound on weak points; a distinct ripple on kill.
- Creatures show damage on the body: wound decals, glowing fluid spurts, popped pustules. Critical state: limping, leaking.
- The player's own damage: screen-edge veins pulse red (red is reserved for damage), directional on the side of the hit.
- Every enemy attack has a readable wind-up of at least 0.35 s with a glow or sound tell.
